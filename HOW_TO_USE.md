# How to Use Your Budget Tracker

## Opening the App

1. Find the `index.html` file in this folder
2. Double-click it to open in your web browser
3. That's it! The app works entirely in your browser - no installation needed!

## Features

### 1. Add Expenses
- Fill out the form at the top:
  - **Description**: What did you buy? (e.g., "Groceries", "Movie tickets")
  - **Amount**: How much did it cost?
  - **Category**: Choose from 7 categories (Food, Entertainment, Bills, etc.)
  - **Date**: When did you spend it? (defaults to today)
- Click "Add Expense" and it appears in your list!

### 2. View Your Spending
- **Total Spent**: See all your expenses added up
- **This Month**: See just this month's spending
- **Recent Expenses**: Scroll through all your expenses
- **Category Breakdown**: See which categories you spend most on with visual bars

### 3. Delete Expenses
- Click "Delete" next to any expense to remove it
- Click "Clear All" to start fresh (careful - this deletes everything!)

### 4. Your Data is Saved
- Everything is saved automatically in your browser
- Close the page and come back - your expenses are still there!
- Note: Data is saved per browser, so use the same browser to see your history

## Tips for Beginners

- **Track everything**: Even small purchases add up!
- **Check daily**: Add expenses as you make them
- **Review monthly**: Look at your category breakdown to see spending patterns
- **Set a goal**: Try to keep your monthly total under a certain amount

## What Each File Does (For Learning!)

Even though you don't code yet, here's what's happening behind the scenes:

### index.html
This is the **structure** of your app - like the skeleton of a building. It contains:
- The header and title
- The form where you enter expenses
- The places where expenses will appear
- Links to the styling (CSS) and functionality (JavaScript)

### style.css
This is the **appearance** - like painting and decorating a building. It makes:
- The purple gradient background
- The white cards and rounded corners
- The colorful category tags
- Everything look pretty and organized!

### script.js
This is the **brain** - it makes everything work! It:
- Saves your expenses when you add them
- Shows them in the list
- Calculates the totals
- Deletes expenses when you click delete
- Saves everything to your browser's memory
- Updates the category breakdown bars

## Customization Ideas (For Later!)

Once you learn more, you could:
- Change the colors in `style.css`
- Add more categories in `index.html` and `script.js`
- Add a budget limit warning
- Export expenses to Excel
- Add charts and graphs
- Create monthly reports

## Troubleshooting

**Q: My expenses disappeared!**
A: Did you switch browsers or use incognito mode? Data is saved per browser.

**Q: Can I use this on my phone?**
A: Yes! It works on mobile browsers too. Just open the file in your phone's browser.

**Q: Can I share this with a friend?**
A: Yes! Just send them the whole folder (all 3 files). But note: you won't share the same data - each person has their own tracker.

**Q: Is my financial data private?**
A: Yes! Everything stays on your computer. Nothing is sent to the internet.
