# How to Enable Your Budget Tracker Website (GitHub Pages)

Follow these simple steps to make your budget tracker accessible from any device!

## Step 1: Go to Your Repository Settings

1. Open your web browser and go to: `https://github.com/steeb3/cuddly-sniffle`
2. Click on the **Settings** tab (near the top right)
3. In the left sidebar, click on **Pages** (under "Code and automation")

## Step 2: Enable GitHub Pages

1. Under "Source", click the dropdown that says "None"
2. Select the branch: **claude/brainstorm-project-ideas-HkWPD**
3. Leave the folder as **/ (root)**
4. Click **Save**

## Step 3: Wait a Minute

GitHub will build your website. This takes about 1-2 minutes.

## Step 4: Get Your Website Link

1. Refresh the Pages settings page
2. You'll see a message at the top saying: "Your site is live at `https://steeb3.github.io/cuddly-sniffle/`"
3. Click that link or copy it!

## Step 5: Use on Your iPhone

1. Open Safari on your iPhone
2. Go to: `https://steeb3.github.io/cuddly-sniffle/`
3. Tap the Share button (square with arrow)
4. Choose "Add to Home Screen"
5. Now it appears like a real app on your iPhone!

## That's It!

Your budget tracker is now:
- Accessible from any device
- Works like a real website
- Can be bookmarked or added to your phone's home screen
- Available at: `https://steeb3.github.io/cuddly-sniffle/`

## Important Notes

- Your expense data is saved separately on each device/browser you use
- The website updates automatically when you make changes to the code
- It's completely free and stays live as long as the repository exists
